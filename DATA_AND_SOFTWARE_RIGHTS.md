# Data and software rights

This public package publishes the research code, protocols, recorded notebooks, paper, aggregate analytical results and synthetic examples. Public access to a source and permission to redistribute its complete records are different questions.

- HistData and Dukascopy source archives, processed minute panels and full event-path arrays remain outside this public package. A public redistribution licence for those snapshots has not been established.
- Per-event prediction records and source-return cross-check tables are also omitted. Published statistical summaries and research figures are retained as the analytical record.
- USMPD is attributed to its authors and the Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco. Obtain the source workbook from its publisher; it is not mirrored here.
- Raw-file manifests retain provenance, file sizes and hashes without embedding the source files. These support comparison with separately acquired inputs.
- Executed notebooks are historical research records. Full empirical reruns require local input snapshots as described in `data/README.md`.
- Original code, prose and website design are credited to Joel Cerraga. No general open-source licence is assigned in this release.
- Plotly.js 3.1.0 is distributed under its MIT licence in `vendor/plotly-LICENSE.txt`. Other retained third-party notices remain in `vendor/`.
- The two cover thumbnails are conceptual artwork generated with the built-in image tool. They are not research figures. Prompts are recorded in `assets/thumbnails/prompts.json`; graph previews are generated directly from analytical results by `scripts/build_site.py`.

The packaging decision does not change the underlying research conclusions or grant rights to third-party inputs.
